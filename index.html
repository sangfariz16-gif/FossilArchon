FAQ

The short answers.

How does the mint work?

Every mint draws one token at random from the pool of 501. You do not choose the artwork — the contract picks it, and the reveal shows what you got. First come, first served.

What is the one-of-one?

The Primordial Genesis. There is only one in the pool of 501, so only one wallet in the world can ever hold it.

How do I get the free mint?

33 free mints exist, one per wallet. Do the five support steps on the launch post first — like, repost, comment, quote, follow. The steps are requested, not checked on-chain: the contract cannot verify X actions.

What does a paid mint cost?

0.0003 BNB per token, plus network gas. There is no per-wallet limit on paid mints, and there is no limit per transaction.

Which wallet can I use?

Any wallet on BNB Smart Chain — MetaMask, Binance Wallet, or an AI agent wallet like the Binance Agentic Wallet. Make sure you are on BNB Smart Chain (chain 56).

When does minting open?

Minting is open now — the contract is live on BNB Smart Chain.

Is this an investment?

No. It is a collectible. Nothing here promises a return.
